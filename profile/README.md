The home of independent *NIX development.
We believe the operating system should serve the user, not the corporation.

The NIX Foundation is an open-source organization dedicated to preserving the philosophy of low-level computing, free software, and the *NIX legacy. We stand against telemetry, bloatware, and the over-commercialization of the UNIX name.

## Our Mission

- Freedom over Monopoly: We believe that operating systems should be built by the community, for the community.
- Low-Level Excellence: We support projects that interact directly with hardware, from bootloaders to custom kernels.
- Zero Telemetry: Software should not spy on its users. Privacy is a fundamental right.
- Open Standards: The *NIX ecosystem belongs to everyone, not to a single certification board.
